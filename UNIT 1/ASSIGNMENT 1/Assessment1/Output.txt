ASYMPTOTIC PROOF
f(n) = 3n² + 10n log2(n) + 50
For n >= 2, log2(n) <= n.
Therefore, f(n) <= 3n² + 10n² + 50n² = 63n².
Also, f(n) >= 3n².
Choose c1=3, c2=63, n0=2.
Thus, f(n) = Θ(n²).
It is not O(n), because f(n)/n grows without bound.
